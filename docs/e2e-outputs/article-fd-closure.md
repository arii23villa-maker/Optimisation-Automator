# Guide to Fixed Deposit Closure and Premature FD Closure

Managing your investments effectively includes understanding how to access your funds when needed. Whether your Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit (FD) has reached maturity or you require an early withdrawal, the process is designed to be transparent and accessible.

Bajaj Finance Limited is a deposit-taking Non-Banking Financial Company (a financial firm that provides banking services but lacks a full banking license) registered with the Reserve Bank of India. Please note that deposits with NBFCs are not insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (the body that insures bank deposits in India).

## What is the process for premature FD closure?

Closing a Fixed Deposit account with Bajaj Finance is a straightforward process that can be managed either upon maturity or prematurely if your financial needs change. Whether you are withdrawing at the end of your chosen tenure or requesting an early closure, preparation is key to a smooth experience.

It is important to note that premature closure of an FD may attract penalties. This could impact the total interest earned.

Bajaj Finance provides clear pathways to facilitate these transactions, allowing you to access your capital efficiently. Our predictable returns model ensures you understand your earnings throughout the tenure.

## How do I complete my FD closure application?

Closing a Fixed Deposit with Bajaj Finance can be conveniently done online through our digital portal or by visiting a branch in person. To complete your fd closure application online, follow these steps:

Once your request is submitted, Bajaj Finance will process it, and the funds will be credited to your linked bank account. This method offers a seamless way to manage your fixed deposit closures without needing to visit a branch.

- Access the portal: Visit the Bajaj Finance customer portal by clicking the sign-in button on this page.
- Login: Enter your registered mobile number. You will receive a One-Time Password (OTP) on your mobile phone; enter this to verify your identity.
- Verify details: Enter your Date of Birth (DOB) to confirm you are the account holder and click on Raise a Request.
- Select product: Choose FD as your product and select the specific FD number you wish to close.
- Specify query: In the query type section, select Prematurity, then choose Need Prematurity Details as your sub-query.
- Submit request: Review all details for accuracy and submit your request.

## What happens at the maturity of your Fixed Deposit?

As your FD approaches maturity, Bajaj Finance will notify you regarding the maturity date and the total amount due. You have the flexibility to decide how to proceed with your funds once the term concludes.

If no specific instructions are provided, the FD may automatically renew for the same tenure at the prevailing interest rate. This ensures your money continues to grow without interruption.

Alternatively, if you wish to withdraw the funds, you must submit formal maturity instructions. Upon receipt of these instructions, we will disburse the principal amount along with the accrued interest directly to your linked bank account.

## What documentation is required for closure?

To finalize your closure request, specific documentation is necessary to verify the account and the holder's identity. Providing these documents promptly helps avoid processing delays.

The required documents include:

- Fixed Deposit Receipt: The original document issued during creation, serving as proof of deposit and including details like amount, tenure, and interest rate.
- FD Certificate: Provides comprehensive details including terms and conditions, interest rates, and maturity dates.
- Identity Proof: A valid document such as a PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport, or driver’s license.
- Account Closure Form: Required for branch-based requests; this form must be filled out and signed to authenticate the request.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Below are common questions regarding our Fixed Deposit services:

### What happens if I close my Fixed Deposit prematurely?

If you close your FD prematurely, you will receive the principal amount plus interest earned up to the date of closure. The interest rate applied will be lower than the original contracted rate, typically reflecting the rate for the period the deposit was actually held.

### What are the penalty charges for premature FD closure?

For withdrawals made before 6 months, no interest is earned. If you withdraw after 6 months, a penalty involves a 2% reduction from the interest rate applicable for the tenure the deposit was held.

### Can the FD account be broken without any penalty?

Generally, premature closure of an FD incurs penalty charges. However, there may be specific schemes or exceptional circumstances, such as medical emergencies, where rules vary; it is recommended to contact customer service to check your specific policy.

### How do I get an FD receipt?

You can download your FD receipt by logging into the Bajaj Finance customer portal. Once logged in, navigate to the Fixed Deposits section to access and download your document.
