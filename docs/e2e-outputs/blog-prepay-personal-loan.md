# How to Prepay Your Personal Loan with Bajaj Finance

A personal loan prepayment allows you to pay off your loan early, potentially reducing your total interest burden. Bajaj Finance Limited, an RBI (Reserve Bank of India)-registered NBFC (non-banking financial company), offers flexible options for part-prepayment or full prepayment of your ongoing loan.

This guide explains the simple steps to process your prepayment through our digital service portal. Last Updated: May 22, 2024.

## What is the personal loan prepayment procedure?

A personal loan prepayment is the act of paying off a portion or the entirety of your loan before the scheduled tenure ends. This process allows you to manage your debt effectively. Bajaj Finance Limited, an RBI-registered NBFC, offers a straightforward digital portal to handle these transactions. Loan approval is at the lender's discretion and subject to eligibility and documentation. All interest rates, fees, and charges are subject to change. Please refer to your Key Fact Statement (KFS) and the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for personalized details.

If you are considering a loan, you may be eligible for a pre-approved offer ranging from Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 55 lakh. You can check your eligibility online by entering your mobile number and OTP without needing to visit a branch.

## Understanding Part-Prepayment and Eligibility for Bajaj Finance Personal Loan

Part-prepayment allows you to reduce your principal outstanding by making payments toward your loan before the due date. This feature is available for ongoing Bajaj Finance Personal Loans, letting you prepay your EMIs up to six times a year. To qualify for a part-prepayment, you must have cleared your first EMI. Each transaction must be at least equal to three EMIs. There is no upper limit on the amount you can pay. You can use our personal loan repayment calculator to see how these payments affect your overall loan schedule.

## Steps to Make Part Payment of your loan in advance

You can initiate a part-prepayment securely through our customer service portal by following these steps:

Visit the Bajaj Finance service portal and click the Sign-in button.

Enter your mobile number and date of birth, then verify your login using the OTP sent to your registered number.

After signing in, navigate to the Service section and select Relations.

Choose the specific loan account number you wish to prepay.

Click on Make loan payments and select the Part-prepayment option.

Enter the desired amount, review the applicable charges, and select your preferred payment mode to complete the transaction.

Alternatively, you can select the Pay a part of your loan option directly on the portal to proceed.

## What are the loan types and their prepayment features?

Bajaj Finance Limited offers three distinct loan products, each with unique withdrawal and repayment structures.

### Flexi Hybrid Loan

This product allows you to withdraw from your sanctioned limit and prepay as often as you like without extra charges. For the first 36 months, you pay interest-only EMIs, after which EMIs include both principal and interest. EMI is applicable only on the withdrawn amount. Flexi facility charges apply based on your loan amount.

### Flexi Dropline Loan

With this option, you can withdraw and prepay multiple times without additional charges. You pay fixed EMIs that cover both principal and interest on the amount withdrawn. Flexi facility charges apply based on your loan amount.

### Term Loan

The full loan amount is disbursed at the start. You pay fixed EMIs covering both principal and interest throughout the tenure. No Flexi facility charges apply to this loan type.
