# How Salaried Employees Can Get a Digital Personal Loan

Salaried employees can obtain a digital personal loan from Bajaj Finance Limited by completing a streamlined online application process through our website or mobile app.

## Understanding the Digital Loan Process and Bajaj Finance Personal Loan

A digital loan is a modern funding instrument available through digital lending platforms such as mobile apps or websites. The most popular form of digital loan today is a personal loan, which is an unsecured credit option that does not require the borrower to provide any collateral as security. This form of financing is ideal for individuals who need funds quickly to meet emergency financial needs or manage unanticipated costs that may be difficult to cover with regular monthly income alone.

Bajaj Finance Limited, an RBI-registered NBFC and Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuer, offers a Bajaj Finance personal loan of up to ₹55 lakh. This option provides a simple application process that you can complete from the comfort of your home. Whether you are managing planned expenses like travel and children’s education or handling hefty payments, this loan is available to any salaried employee working in a private or public organization who meets the lender's eligibility requirements.

When reviewing your loan, always refer to the Key Fact Statement (KFS)—a document summarizing your loan's true cost—and the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), which represents the total yearly cost of the loan including interest and fees. Please note that interest rates, fees, and charges are subject to change, and loan approval is at the lender's discretion, subject to eligibility and documentation.

## How to Apply for a Digital Personal Loan for Salaried Employees

Any salaried professional who meets the basic personal loan eligibility criteria can apply for a Bajaj Finance personal loan online. Once you meet the necessary criteria, you will need to submit specific personal loan documents to complete your application. Follow this step-by-step guide to apply for a digital personal loan:

After your documents have been verified and approved, the loan amount will be credited directly to your bank account. The entire process is designed to be quick and hassle-free.

- Visit the personal loan page on our website.
- Click the APPLY button at the top of the page to launch the online application form.
- Enter your 10-digit mobile phone number and verify it using the OTP sent to your device.
- Fill out the application form with your personal details, including your full name, PAN, date of birth, and PIN code.
- Click PROCEED to navigate to the loan selection page.
- Enter your desired loan amount and select from our available personal loan variants.
- Choose a repayment term ranging from 12 months to 108 months, then click PROCEED.
- Complete your KYC requirements digitally and submit your application.

## Comparing Digital Loans and Regular Personal Loan Options

Digital loans offer significant advantages over traditional offline methods, particularly regarding speed, accessibility, and convenience. While offline applications require physical visits to a lender's office to submit forms and documents—which can be time-consuming and overwhelming—digital platforms allow you to upload documents electronically and manage your loan entirely online.

With an online personal loan, you can conveniently look at multiple loan options simultaneously, allowing you to choose the best loan for your needs without having to visit the office of each lender. You can also take advantage of the personal loan EMI calculator to quickly figure out how much you must pay towards your loan each month.

Regarding processing time, there is a large difference between the two methods. Online applications can be approved instantly, and the loan disbursal is very quick, with funds available within 24 hours of approval. In contrast, offline applications require in-person document submission, and the verification process may take longer.

### Documents Required

Online personal loans require minimal documentation that can be submitted electronically. Please ensure you have the following ready:

- KYC documents: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, or voter’s ID
- Employee ID card
- Salary slips for the last 3 months
- Bank account statements for the previous 3 months
