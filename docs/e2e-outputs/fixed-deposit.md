# Grow Your Savings Securely with a Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit

A Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit (FD) is a secure investment instrument that allows you to grow your savings with predictable returns. By investing a lump sum for a fixed tenure, you benefit from steady earnings unaffected by market volatility.

With interest rates up to 8.15% p.a. For senior citizens and up to 7.75% p.a. For other individuals, our FDs offer a reliable way to build wealth. You can open an FD online with as little as ₹15,000 through a fully paperless process.

## Why Choose a Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit for Senior Citizens and Others?

Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposits offer a secure way to grow your savings with predictable returns. As an RBI-registered (Reserve Bank of India), deposit-taking NBFC (Non-Banking Financial Company), Bajaj Finance Limited has a proven track record of timely repayment.

Our deposits are backed by the highest safety ratings, ensuring peace of mind for conservative investors. Please note that deposits with NBFCs are not insured by DICGC.

Our commitment to financial stability is reflected in our top-tier credit ratings. We are rated [ICRA]AAA(Stable) and CRISIL AAA/STABLE, which indicate the highest degree of certainty regarding the timely payment of interest and principal.

- Highest Safety Ratings: Our FDs are rated [ICRA]AAA(Stable) and CRISIL AAA/STABLE, reflecting the highest level of creditworthiness.
- Predictable Returns: Know exactly what you will earn from the day you book, with interest rates locked for your entire tenure.
- Quick Booking: Benefit from a 1-hour booking window upon receipt of funds, subject to successful document verification.
- Flexible Payouts: Choose from cumulative or non-cumulative options, including monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly interest payouts.

## Features, Benefits, and Interest Rates

Our fixed deposits are designed to provide flexibility and convenience, catering to both individuals and corporate entities. Whether you are looking for regular income or long-term growth, our features support your financial goals.

We offer interest rates of up to 8.15% p.a. For senior citizens and up to 7.75% p.a. For other individuals. You can start your investment with as little as ₹15,000 and choose tenures ranging from 12 to 60 months.

The interest rate is locked at the time of booking and remains fixed for the entire tenure, protecting you from market fluctuations.

- High Interest Rates: Earn up to 8.15% p.a., with an additional benefit of up to 0.40% p.a. For senior citizens.
- Flexible Tenures: Select any investment period ranging from 12 to 60 months.
- Digital Management: Use our dedicated customer portal, Service, to download your Fixed Deposit Receipt (FDR), Interest Certificate (IC), and Statement of Accounts (SOA).
- Loan Against FD: Access liquidity by obtaining a loan against your deposit, with up to 75% of your investment amount for cumulative FDs and up to 60% for non-cumulative FDs.
- Clear Premature-Withdrawal Rules: You may close your FD after the completion of 3 months from the interest start date, subject to a penalty as per RBI guidelines.

## Eligibility and Documentation

We have streamlined our eligibility criteria to ensure a wide range of investors can participate. Whether you are an individual, a senior citizen, a corporate entity, or a trust, you can book an FD with us.

Minors can also open an FD through a parent or legal guardian. Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Persons of Indian Origin (PIO), and Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) are eligible to invest and should contact our representative or email wecare@bajajfinserv.in for assistance.

- Investor Types: Resident Indians, NRIs, PIOs, OCIs, Corporate entities, Individuals, Senior Citizens, Minors, HUFs, Sole Proprietorships, Partnership Firms, Clubs, Associations, Societies, and Family Trusts.
- Required Documents: A valid PAN (Permanent Account Number) is mandatory, along with a KYC (Know Your Customer) document such as an Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving License, or Voter ID.
- Digital KYC: Complete your verification seamlessly via CKYC (Central KYC), OKYC (Offline KYC), or Video-KYC (VCIP - Video Customer Identification Process).

## How to Open FD Online or Renew

Investing online is designed to be effortless. Visit our website or mobile app, navigate to 'Investments,' select 'Fixed Deposit,' and follow the prompts to enter your details and complete your KYC.

Once your payment via NetBanking, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS is successful, your FD is booked within one hour. You will receive an acknowledgement immediately, and an e-FDR will be sent to your email within 3 working days.

You can renew your FD up to 24 hours before maturity through our customer portal, Service. You have the flexibility to choose between 'Principal,' 'Principal + Interest,' or 'Partial Renewal' options.

## Taxation and Disclaimers

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) at 10% is deducted on interest income exceeding ₹10,000 in a financial year. If you do not provide a PAN, TDS is deducted at 20%.

If your total income is below the taxable limit, you may submit Form 15G (for individuals under 60) or Form 15H (for senior citizens) to receive interest without deduction. TDS is adjusted against your final tax liability.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a fixed deposit (FD)?

A fixed deposit is a savings instrument where you invest a lump sum for a specific tenure at a fixed interest rate. It offers predictable returns, making it a stable choice compared to market-linked investments.

### What is the tenure for investing in a Bajaj Finance FD?

Bajaj Finance offers flexible tenure options ranging from 12 months to 60 months. You can choose the duration that best aligns with your financial goals.

### Can I withdraw from my fixed deposit before maturity?

Yes, premature withdrawal is permitted after the completion of 3 months from the interest start date. Please note that a penalty will be applied as per RBI guidelines.

### How much can be deposited in a fixed deposit?

For online bookings, you can invest between ₹15,000 and ₹3 crore. There is no upper limit for offline investments.

### Is Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit safe?

Yes, it is highly safe. Bajaj Finance FDs are rated [ICRA]AAA(Stable) and CRISIL AAA/STABLE, which are the highest safety grades for corporate deposits.

### Why is an FD considered one of the safest investments?

FDs provide fixed returns that are not impacted by market volatility. With our highest credit ratings, investors can be assured of the certainty of timely interest and principal payments.

### Is a Bajaj Finance FD covered by deposit insurance?

No, deposit insurance only covers bank deposits. Bajaj Finance FDs are secured by the company's strong credit rating and our track record as an RBI-registered NBFC.

### What is the minimum amount for investing in a fixed deposit?

You can start your investment in a Bajaj Finance Fixed Deposit with a minimum amount of ₹15,000.

### Is Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) applicable?

Yes, TDS at 10% is deducted on interest income exceeding ₹10,000 in a financial year. If you do not provide a PAN, the deduction is 20%.

### Can a minor open a Fixed Deposit?

Yes, a minor can open an FD under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. The guardian manages the account until the minor reaches the age of majority.

### What happens if I forget to renew my FD after maturity?

Maturity proceeds are processed according to the instructions provided at the time of booking. It is recommended to monitor your maturity dates to reinvest or book a new FD.

### What are the benefits of a senior citizen FD?

Senior citizens benefit from higher interest rates, currently up to 0.40% p.a. Extra. This helps maximize returns for retirement-focused financial planning.

### How can I calculate my FD returns?

You can use the online FD calculator on our website. Simply enter your investment amount, chosen tenure, and interest rate to view your expected maturity value.

### Does my FD rate change if market rates change after booking?

No. The interest rate is locked on the day of booking and remains constant for the entire tenure of your deposit. Changes in market rates only affect new deposits or renewals.

### How many deposits can I make?

There is no limit to the number of fixed deposits you can hold. You can create an FD ladder by booking multiple deposits with different tenures and amounts.

### Can I get monthly interest payouts?

Yes, you can select the non-cumulative monthly payout scheme. This is ideal for those who prefer to receive regular income from their investment.

### How long does it take to book an FD online?

The online process takes only a few minutes. Once the funds are received, the FD is typically booked within one hour, subject to successful verification.

### How do I manage my FD documents?

You can log in to our customer portal, Service, to view and download your Fixed Deposit Receipt, Interest Certificate, and account statements at any time.

### Are there any hidden charges?

No, our process is transparent. We disclose all terms, including TDS and premature withdrawal penalties, at the time of booking.

### What is the highest interest rate offered?

We offer up to 8.15% p.a. For senior citizens and up to 7.75% p.a. For other individuals. Exact rates depend on the chosen tenure.
