## Eligibility Criteria and Required Documentation

To qualify for a personal loan with Bajaj Finance Limited, applicants must meet specific eligibility criteria. We maintain clear guidelines to ensure a responsible lending process. Loan approval is at the lender's discretion and is subject to eligibility and documentation verification.

Applicants must be Indian citizens between the ages of 21 and 80 years at the end of the loan tenure. You must be employed with a public, private, or MNC entity, or be self-employed. A CIBIL score of 650 or higher is mandatory for eligibility.

- Nationality: Indian.
- Age: 21 to 80 years (at the end of loan tenure).
- Employment: Salaried or self-employed.
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 650.
- KYC Documents: Aadhaar, passport, voter’s ID, driving license, Letter of National Population Register, or NREGA job card.
- Financial Documents: PAN card, salary slips for the last 3 months, and bank account statements for the previous 3 months.
- Additional Documents: Employee ID card, piped gas bill, pension order, Letter of Allotment of Accommodation, property/municipal tax receipt, utility bill, phone bill, or ration card.

## Important Considerations for First-Time Borrowers

As a first-time borrower, your primary goal should be to build a positive credit history while managing debt responsibly. Making informed decisions is essential to maintaining financial health.

- Understand the terms: Read the fine print regarding interest rates, processing fees, and repayment schedules before signing any agreement.
- Check your credit score: A higher score significantly improves your chances of approval and obtaining favorable terms.
- Borrow only what you need: Assess your actual requirement to keep your EMI burden manageable and avoid unnecessary interest costs.
- Compare offers: Research various lenders to ensure you are getting the best deal available regarding interest rates and service quality.
- Understand repayment: Be fully aware of the consequences of late payments, including penal charges, to protect your credit score.
