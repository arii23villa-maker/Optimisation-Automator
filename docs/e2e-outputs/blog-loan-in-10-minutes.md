The emergence of digital lending has transformed the borrowing experience, allowing individuals to secure funds with greater convenience. Our platform provides a transparent lending process for those seeking financial support.

Please note that interest rates, fees, and charges are subject to change. We encourage all applicants to refer to the Key Fact Statement (KFS) and Annual Percentage Rate (APR) provided during the application process for precise details. Loan approval is at the lender's discretion and is subject to eligibility and documentation.

- Quick online process: Apply anytime using our website or app.
- Minimal documentation: An online-first approach reduces the need for extensive paperwork.
- Flexible usage: Use the funds for any legitimate financial requirement.
- No collateral required: These are unsecured loans, meaning you do not need to pledge assets.
- Transparent terms: All fees and charges are clearly mentioned on our website and in your loan documents.

## Eligibility and Documentation

The eligibility criteria for the Bajaj Finance Insta Personal Loan are designed to be inclusive, catering to salaried and self-employed individuals. Lenders consider factors such as your CIBIL Score (a credit score reported by the Credit Information Bureau of India, ranging 300-900), employment status, income, and age.

To ensure a smooth application, please keep the following documents ready for verification.

- Nationality: Indian citizen.
- Age: 21 to 80 years (you must be 80 or younger at the end of the loan tenure).
- Employment: Public, private, or MNC sector.
- Credit Score: CIBIL Score of 650 or higher.
- KYC documents: Aadhaar, passport, voter’s ID, driving license, Letter of National Population Register, or NREGA job card.
- Financial proof: PAN card, salary slips (last 3 months), and bank statements (last 3 months).
- Additional documents: Employee ID card, piped gas bill, pension order, Letter of Allotment of Accommodation, property or municipal tax receipt, utility bill, real-time photograph, and ration card.

## How do I get an immediate personal loan?

Getting an Insta Personal Loan is simple. Our digital interface guides you through the process, which may vary slightly depending on whether you are a new customer or have an existing relationship with Bajaj Finance.

Upon completion, our digital platform employs advanced algorithms to assess the provided information. This helps in expediting the approval process for eligible applicants.

- Visit our website or app and click on CHECK OFFER.
- Enter your 10-digit mobile number and verify it via OTP.
- View your pre-assigned limit or select your preferred loan amount.
- Choose a repayment tenure that aligns with your budget.
- Click PROCEED to finalize your online application.

## EMI Calculator and Repayment

Use our online EMI calculator to estimate your monthly repayment. The calculator shows the amount you will pay each month for the chosen loan amount, tenure, and interest rate.

The loan repayment process is structured through fixed EMIs over a chosen tenure. These include both principal and interest, with reducing balance methods lowering interest over time.

### What credit score is required?

While we offer pre-approved loans, a CIBIL Score of 650 or higher increases your chances of approval. Lenders typically evaluate credit scores alongside other parameters before approving an application.

### What is the interest rate?

Interest rates for Bajaj Finance Insta Personal Loans are subject to change. We encourage you to refer to the Key Fact Statement (KFS) and Annual Percentage Rate (APR) provided during the application process for the exact rate applicable to you.

### Are there hidden fees?

All fees and charges are clearly mentioned on our website and in your loan documents. There are no hidden charges.

### Can I prepay or foreclose my loan?

You can repay the loan early at any time without any prepayment penalty. If you choose to part-pay or foreclose, confirm the terms in your agreement, but there are no additional fees for full early repayment.

### How is the repayment structured?

Repayment is through fixed EMIs over a chosen tenure using a reducing balance method. Each EMI includes both principal and interest components.

### How do I check my eligibility?
