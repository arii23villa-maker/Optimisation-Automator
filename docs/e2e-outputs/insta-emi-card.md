The Bajaj Finance Insta EMI Card, also known as the EMI Network Card, is a powerful financial tool designed to provide flexibility by allowing you to convert purchases into manageable monthly instalments. It is a 100% digital product, meaning you do not need to carry a physical card; instead, you manage your account and access card details directly through the Bajaj Finance app. The card is accepted at over 1.5 lakh partner stores across 4,000+ cities in India, as well as on leading e-commerce platforms.

Whether you are looking to upgrade your electronics, purchase home appliances, or buy furniture, the card simplifies the process. You can shop for over 1 million products, including smartphones, smart TVs, refrigerators, air conditioners, mattresses, laptops, washing machines, smartwatches, speakers, fitness equipment, and cycles. Furthermore, the card offers the freedom to foreclose your loan at any time without extra fees, and you can enjoy zero down payment on select models at partner stores. The application process is entirely online and can be completed in less than 10 minutes.

The Insta EMI Card is designed to be accessible to a wide range of individuals. While Bajaj Finance Limited maintains the sole discretion to accept or reject any application without assigning a reason, you must meet the following basic criteria to be eligible for the card.

## How to Apply Online

The application process for the Insta EMI Card is fully digital and can be completed in under 10 minutes. Please note that the exact steps may differ slightly depending on whether you are a new customer or if you already have an existing relationship with Bajaj Finance.

To start, visit the official Bajaj Finance website and follow these steps to secure your card loan offer.

- Enter your 10-digit mobile number and click on 'GET IT NOW'.
- Submit the OTP received on your mobile to proceed.
- Enter your full name and select 'Yes, Auto-Fetch Details' to pull your information.
- Verify your pre-filled personal details, select your gender and employment type, and click 'CONTINUE'.
- View your pre-qualified card loan offer amount and click 'CONTINUE'.
- Complete your identity verification by confirming your current residential address.
- Review your wallet setup details and verify with an OTP.
- Pay the one-time joining fee of Rs. 530 (inclusive of applicable taxes).
- Once payment is successful, access your digital card details through the Bajaj Finance app.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Bajaj Finance Insta EMI Card?

The Insta EMI Card, also known as the EMI Network Card, is a digital credit facility from Bajaj Finance Limited. It allows you to convert the cost of purchases into Easy EMIs at partner stores and online retailers.

### Is the Bajaj Pay Wallet required for the Insta EMI Card?

Yes, creating or activating a Bajaj Pay Wallet is required during the application process. This wallet is used to receive your cashback rewards and manage your transactions.

### What is the joining fee for the card?

The Insta EMI Card has a one-time joining fee of Rs. 530, which is inclusive of all applicable taxes. There are no annual or renewal charges thereafter.

### What are the eligibility age requirements?

To apply for the Insta EMI Card, you must be between 21 and 70 years of age.

### Do I get a physical card?

No, the Insta EMI Card is a completely digital product. You can access your card details and manage your account through the Bajaj Finance app.

### Where can I use my Insta EMI Card?

You can use your card at over 1.5 lakh partner stores across 4,000 cities in India. It is also accepted on various leading e-commerce websites.

### What documents do I need to apply?

You do not need to submit physical documents. You simply need your PAN card details and your Aadhaar card number for online KYC verification.

### How do I register for an e-mandate?

To register your e-mandate, you must provide your bank account number and IFSC code via the portal. You will then need to submit an OTP to validate the bank details for auto-debit payments.

### What are the benefits of e-mandate registration?

Registering for an e-mandate ensures you never miss an EMI payment due to the auto-debit feature. It also helps in managing your loan repayments more efficiently.

### Why is the Bajaj Finance app necessary?

The app is your primary tool for managing your card, viewing your loan details, and accessing exclusive offers. It also allows you to view your digital card for transactions.

### Can I use the card at offline stores?

Yes, you can visit any of the Bajaj Finance partner retail stores and provide your card details at the billing counter to finance your purchase.

### What happens if I cannot transact with my card?

If you are unable to transact, your card might be blocked. You can check your card status and the reason for the block by signing into the 'My Account' portal or the Bajaj Finance app.

### How can I unblock my card?

You can unblock your card by logging into the 'My Account' portal. Select your card under 'My Relations', choose the 'Unblock Card' option in 'Quick Actions', and verify the process with an OTP.

### When is my cashback credited?

Your assured welcome cashback is credited to your Bajaj Pay Wallet after the successful payment of your card joining fee. Future rewards are credited based on the specific offer terms.

### How can I contact customer care?

You can reach out for support through the 'My Account' customer portal. Alternatively, you can call the customer care team at +91 8698010101.

### What rewards can I earn with this card?

Cardholders are eligible for a flat Rs. 1,000 cashback on their first in-store transaction. You may also receive assured cashback of up to Rs. 50 and complimentary brand vouchers.

### Does the card have annual charges?

No, there are no annual or renewal charges for the Insta EMI Card. You only pay the one-time joining fee of Rs. 530.

### Can I foreclose my loan early?

Yes, you have the freedom to foreclose your loan anytime. Foreclosure and part-payment charges are 4.72% for interest-bearing schemes and nil for non-interest-bearing schemes.

### How do I view my card details in the app?

Open the Bajaj Finance app, log in using your mobile number and OTP, then enter your date of birth. Click on the 'EMI Card' icon to view your details.

### What products can I buy with the card?

You can use the card to purchase a wide range of products including smartphones, laptops, smart TVs, home appliances, furniture, fitness equipment, and even groceries.
