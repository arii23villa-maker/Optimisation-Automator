The Flexi Dropline Loan provides a sanctioned limit from which you can withdraw funds as and when needed. It's an excellent choice for ongoing financial needs or situations where you might need access to funds at different intervals.

The Term Loan is a traditional personal loan where the entire sanctioned amount is disbursed to you at once. You then repay this amount through fixed monthly EMIs over a predetermined tenure. ## Calculate Your Personal Loan EMIs

Understanding your Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is crucial for effective financial planning. Bajaj Finance Limited provides an easy-to-use online EMI calculator that helps you estimate your monthly payments based on the principal loan amount, interest rate, and chosen repayment tenure.

The standard formula for calculating EMI is: EMI = P × R × (1 + R)ⁿ / [(1 + R)ⁿ – 1], where P is the principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the tenure in months. Our calculator simplifies this, allowing you to quickly determine an EMI that fits your budget and view a detailed repayment schedule, helping you make informed decisions before applying.
